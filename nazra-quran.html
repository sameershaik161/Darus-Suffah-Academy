The Art of Reciting with Tartil

✦

Allah commands in Surah Al-Muzzammil: "And recite the Quran with measured, rhythmic recitation." Our Nazra program guides students from reading slow, segmented words to fluid, heartfelt verses.

Led by Qaris holding traditional Sanad certifications, this course takes the student through the complete Quran page by page. We meticulously correct hidden errors (Lahn Khafi) such as inadequate Madd stretching, incorrect nasalization on Noon and Meem Mushaddad, and breath-holding errors at stopping signs.

Who This Course Is For

  • ✔ Graduates of Noorani Qaida ready to begin reading directly from the Mushaf.
  • ✔ Adults who read Arabic with hesitation and want to achieve effortless fluency.
  • ✔ Sisters seeking female Qarias to perfect their recitation for daily prayers.
  • ✔ Children preparing for the comprehensive Hifz (memorization) track.

Mastery Achieved

Key Learning Outcomes

✦

Transform your recitation from mechanical reading into a soul-stirring worship experience.

Noon & Meem Sakin Rules

Intuitive mastery of Izhar, Idgham (with and without Ghunnah), Iqlab, and Ikhfa across any verse in the Quran.

Madd (Elongation) Rules

Know when to stretch 2, 4, 5, or 6 counts: Madd Asli, Madd Muttasil, Madd Munfasil, and Madd Lazim.

Rules of Waqf (Stopping)

Decipher all Quranic punctuation marks (Meem, Jeem, Qaf-Laam, Taa, Sad) and learn how to pause and resume without altering meaning.

Heavy & Light Letters (Tafkheem/Tarqeeq)

Master the subtleties of pronouncing the letter Raa (ر) and the Majestic Name of Allah (Lafz al-Jalalah) with proper weight.

Full 30 Juz Khatam

Complete a full reading of the Holy Quran from Surah Al-Fatiha to Surah An-Nas directly under the ears of your tutor.

Curriculum Journey

Nazra Program Roadmap

✦

Organized in 4 quarters guaranteeing thorough feedback on every single Juz.

Stage 1: Juz 30 (Amma) & Foundation Rules

Months 1 - 2

Starting with short Surahs from Juz 30. Direct practical application of Noon Sakin and Tanween rules. Breath control and rhythm training.

Stage 2: Juz 1 to 10 (Surah Al-Baqarah to At-Tawbah)

Months 3 - 5

Navigating longer verses and complex grammatical stops. Detailed study of Meem Sakin and Madd elongation types.

Stage 3: Juz 11 to 20 (Surah Yunus to Al-Anbiya)

Months 6 - 8

Building stamina and consistent pace. Correction of minor vocal slips (Lahn Khafi) and practicing Sajdah Tilawat rules.

Stage 4: Juz 21 to 29 & Grand Khatam Dua

Months 9 - 12

Finalizing the entire Quran. Special Khatam-ul-Quran session with student's family on live video call followed by supplication (Dua).

Weekly Routine

Sample Weekly Schedule

Day 1 (e.g. Tuesday)

Day 2 (e.g. Thursday)

Fluency & Speed Correction

Day 3 (e.g. Saturday)

Quarterly Review & Meaning Context

Reviewing a full quarter of a Juz. Teacher shares brief context and translation of inspiring verses to connect heart and tongue.
