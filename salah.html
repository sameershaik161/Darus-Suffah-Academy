At Darus Suffah Academy, we believe "Baccha sirf yaad nahi karta, samajhta hai." We don't force children to mechanically memorize postures — we teach the beauty, tranquility, and meaning behind every Bowing (Ruku) and Prostration (Sujud).

Students learn the practical Sunnah method: how to perform accurate Wudu, face the Qiblah, pronounce the Takbeerat, recite Surah Al-Fatiha with correct Tajweed, recite Tashahhud, Durood-e-Ibrahim, and Dua-e-Qunoot with understanding.

Who This Course Is For

What the Student Will Learn

✦

Wudu (Ablution) Mastery

The compulsory (Fard) and Sunnah steps of Wudu, what nullifies Wudu, and the Duas recited before and after ablution.

All 5 Daily Prayers

Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha. Number of Rakat, Fard, Sunnah Mu'akkadah, and Witr prayer execution.

Tashahhud & Durood

Flawless memorization and Arabic pronunciation of Attahiyyat, Durood Ibrahim, and Rabbana Duas with word meanings.

Khushu (Focus & Devotion)

Eliminating distractions and understanding the conversation between the servant and Allah during Surah Al-Fatiha.

Special Occasion Prayers

Step-by-step guidance on Salatul Janazah (Funeral Prayer), Eid Salah with extra Takbeerat, and Taraweeh prayers.

Salah Certificate

Awarded an official Darus Suffah Academy Salah Completion Certificate upon passing the practical demonstration test.

Structured Roadmap

Salah Course Syllabus

✦

Module 1: Taharah (Purity), Wudu & Adhan

Weeks 1 - 2

The spiritual conditions before standing for prayer: Cleanliness, clothes, facing Qiblah, step-by-step Wudu, Adhan & Iqamah listening.

Module 2: The Physical Postures of Salah

Weeks 3 - 4

Takbeer Tahreema, Qiyam (standing), Ruku (bowing), Qawmah (rising), Sujud (prostration), Jalsah (sitting between sujud), and Salam.

Module 3: Recitations & Supplications in Prayer

Weeks 5 - 7

Thana (Subhanaka Allahumma), Surah Al-Fatiha with Tajweed, Tasbeeh of Ruku & Sujud, Tashahhud (Attahiyyat), Durood Ibrahim, and Masnoon concluding Duas.

Module 4: 2, 3 & 4 Rakat Prayers & Practical Assessment

Weeks 8 - 10

Putting it into complete practice for Fajr (2 Rakat), Maghrib (3 Rakat), and Dhuhr/Asr/Isha (4 Rakat). Live camera demonstration and certificate exam.
