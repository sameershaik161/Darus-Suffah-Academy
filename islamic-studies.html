Curriculum Focus

Key Pillars of the Program

✦

Aqeedah (Core Islamic Beliefs)

The 6 Pillars of Iman: Tawheed, Angels, Revealed Books, Prophets, the Hereafter, and Qadar (Divine Decree).

Seerah an-Nabawiyyah

The inspiring life story of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ: his noble birth, revelation in Cave Hira, Hijrah to Madinah, and mercy to all mankind.

Stories of the Prophets

Lessons from Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Yusuf, Musa, and Isa (peace be upon them all) on patience, courage, and trust in Allah.

Akhlaq & Social Manners (Adab)

Respecting parents, avoiding backbiting, honesty in school and trade, controlling temper, and showing kindness to all people.

Halal & Haram in Daily Life

Clear understanding of dietary guidelines, pure earnings, modesty in dress, and safeguarding one's digital interactions.

Graduation Certificate

Official Darus Suffah Academy Islamic Studies Certificate awarded upon completing oral and written modular reviews.
