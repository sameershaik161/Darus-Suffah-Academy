Prophetic Living

Bringing the Sunnah into Everyday Life

✦

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "I was only sent to perfect good character." While reciting the Quran illuminates the mind, practicing the Hadith of Rasulullah ﷺ transforms conduct and morals.

This specialized short course is designed to instill essential Islamic values in young students and busy adults. Drawing from classical collections (such as Imam Nawawi's 40 Hadith and authentic Masnoon Dua compendiums), students memorize concise prophetic sayings alongside their practical life applications: how to speak truthfully, respect parents, treat neighbors kindly, control anger, and invoke Allah's protection throughout the day.

Who This Course Is For

  • ✔ Children and teenagers living in Western or metropolitan cities seeking strong Islamic moral foundations.
  • ✔ Parents wanting their children to memorize daily protective Azkar and authentic prayers.
  • ✔ New Muslims wanting an accessible, warm overview of prophetic character and etiquette.

4 Structured Modules

Course Syllabus

✦

Organized logically into progressive monthly milestones.

Module 1: Daily Routine Duas & Foundations of Faith

Weeks 1 - 4

Duas before and after sleep, eating, drinking, bathroom etiquettes, entering and leaving home. Ahadith 1 to 10 covering Ikhlas (Intentions), the Pillars of Islam, and cleanliness.

Module 2: Character, Truthfulness & Social Manners

Weeks 5 - 8

Duas for traveling, sneezing, wearing new clothes, and entering the market. Ahadith 11 to 20 covering kindness to parents, controlling anger, guarding the tongue, and maintaining honesty in friendships.

Module 3: Worship Duas & Compassion to Humanity

Weeks 9 - 12

Duas in and after Salah, Dua-e-Qunoot (Witr), and Dua when hearing thunder or rain. Ahadith 21 to 30 covering mercy towards animals, feeding the hungry, visiting the sick, and brotherhood.

Module 4: Protection, Forgiveness & Final Evaluation

Weeks 13 - 16

Sayyidul Istighfar (The Master Supplication for Forgiveness), protective Ayatul Kursi, and 3 Quls. Ahadith 31 to 40 on preparing for the Day of Judgment, seeking knowledge, and the virtues of the Quran.

Weekly Rhythm

Sample Weekly Schedule

✦

Session 1

New Hadith Memorization

Student learns 1 short Hadith with Arabic pronunciation and Urdu/English translation. Teacher shares an engaging historical story from Seerah illustrating the Hadith.

Session 2

Daily Masnoon Dua Practice

Reciting the new Dua with exact Tajweed articulation. Discussion on the practical real-life moment when this Dua should be invoked.

Session 3

Review & Character Reflection

Oral quiz of all previous Duas learned to date. Student shares how they applied the Sunnah lesson in school, work, or at home during the past week.
